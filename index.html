About

Some people call me a sentient glitch. I prefer error-based human: hand me something broken and I get weirdly happy. Not because I like chaos, but because every error message is a free hint about how the thing really works.

That curiosity became about two years of lab work, a Pro Hacker rank on HTB, and an HTB CPTS. Mostly web apps and Active Directory, and lately AI, which fails in surprisingly familiar ways. The part I care about most comes after the break: writing it up clearly enough that someone can actually fix it. I report with SysReptor and let Python handle the boring parts.

Next goal is bug bounty hunting. Until then I’m open to junior pentesting, vulnerability assessment, and security analyst roles.
